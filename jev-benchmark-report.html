Jev vs an open Jev clone: accuracy benchmark

We benchmarked TypeSafe's hosted Jev model, then self-hosted an open-weights Jev-style model on one H100 and ran the identical test on it. The clone matches Jev on simple classification but is 8 to 10 points behind on knowledge and reasoning.

01What we measured

Jev is a decisions model. It does not write text. It receives some context and a structured question (pick one option, answer yes/no, or rate on a scale) and returns probabilities. We therefore measure accuracy from its chosen answer, and how well its confidence matches reality from the probabilities.

Jev is accurate and well calibrated: its stated confidence is within about 1 to 4 points of its real accuracy on most tasks. The exception is Yelp stars, where it is overconfident by 9 points (it is right 68.8% of the time but 77.8% sure). On ratings it is off by 0.34 stars on average, and 97.9% of its answers are within one star. When the options were reordered, it gave the same answer 96.6% of the time.

03Unofficial model: autotrust/JEV-27B

TypeSafe has not released Jev's weights. We chose autotrust/JEV-27B because it is the closest open replica we found: a third-party model, explicitly not affiliated with TypeSafe, distilled from hosted Jev 1.13 and built to output the same three answer types.

Diff is JEV-27B minus official. Red marks a statistically significant gap (p < 0.05, McNemar exact test). “Same answer” is how often both models picked the same option.

Where the clone holds up. ARC Challenge and SST-2 are within noise (−0.6 and −0.7 points, p above 0.2). Yelp is 1.7 points lower and BoolQ 2.4 lower: statistically real, but small.
Where it falls short. MMLU (−8.2), TruthfulQA (−10.3) and the MMLU position test (−10.2) are large gaps. On MMLU, official Jev was right and the clone wrong on 1,412 questions, against only 265 the other way. The clone is also less stable when answer options are reordered.
Against the model card. The JEV-27B card reports a six-benchmark average on par with hosted Jev (84.07% vs 83.85%, self-reported). We did not reproduce that on knowledge-heavy tasks.

05Limits of this report

  • The unofficial run was done by a teammate on rented hardware. We checked that the result files are complete and that the comparison reproduces from them, but we did not re-run the model.
  • JEV-27B was compared against Jev 1.14, while it was distilled from 1.13. Some of the gap may be a version difference.
  • Accuracy only. Throughput and latency of the self-hosted model are measured separately.
  • Image understanding is untested for the clone. A vision variant, autotrust/JEV-27B-VL, exists.
  • One open model was tested. Smaller options such as alibiserikbay/JevK5 (4B) are untested.
Source: Runaraai/Jev-benchmark (code, raw per-question results and reports). Run settings: seed 0, full datasets, decisions API.
